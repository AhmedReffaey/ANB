Intercom & Internal Communication Systems

Seamless Connectivity for Secure and Efficient Environments

Effective communication is the backbone of any safe and well-managed facility. Our Intercom and Internal Communication Systems provide crystal-clear audio and high-definition video links between entry points, departments, and individual units. Whether you are managing a residential complex, a corporate office, or a high-security industrial site, we ensure you are always connected.

System Benefits
  • Visitor Verification:

    See and speak with visitors at the entrance before granting them access.

  • Improved Operational Flow:

    Instant communication between departments reduces downtime and improves coordination.

  • Privacy and Security:

    Encrypted communication channels ensure that your internal conversations remain private.

  • Remote Management:

    Answer your intercom and unlock doors from your smartphone, even when you are away.

  • Integration:

    Seamlessly connects with access control, CCTV, and mobile apps for a unified security experience.

  • Hands-Free Convenience:

    Modern touch-screen and voice-activated units for ease of use.

System Components
  • Outdoor Stations:

    Weather-proof units with cameras, call buttons, and optional RFID readers.

  • Indoor Monitors:

    Slim profile touch-screens or handsets installed in rooms to receive calls and view video.

  • IP Master Stations:

    Centralized units for security desks or receptionists to manage all building calls.

  • Network PoE Switches:

    Power-over-Ethernet switches to connect and power the devices via a single cable.

  • SIP Server / Management Software:

    The digital core that routes calls and manages user permissions.

Places of Use

  • Luxury villas and residential apartment buildings.

  • Corporate offices and reception areas.

  • Hospitals and specialized nursing stations.

  • Schools and educational campuses.

  • High-security industrial and logistics parks.
