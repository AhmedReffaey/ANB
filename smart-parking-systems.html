Smart Parking Systems

Optimized Mobility and Stress-Free Parking Management

Finding a parking spot shouldn't be a challenge. Our Smart Parking Systems utilize advanced sensor technology, automated barriers, and real-time data to streamline the entire parking process. From guidance systems that lead drivers to open spots to automated payment and license plate recognition (ALPR), we help facility managers increase efficiency and improve the visitor experience.

System Benefits
  • Reduced Traffic Congestion:

    Guidance displays direct drivers to available spots, reducing unnecessary circling.

  • Contactless Entry/Exit:

    Automatic Number Plate Recognition (ANPR) allows authorized vehicles to pass without stopping.

  • Revenue Management:

    Automated ticketing and mobile payment integration ensure accurate and secure revenue collection.

  • Space Optimization:

    Data analytics help managers understand peak hours and optimize space allocation.

  • Enhanced Security:

    Continuous monitoring of all entry and exit points with high-definition cameras.

  • Environmental Impact:

    Less time spent idling and searching for parking leads to lower vehicle emissions in your facility.

System Components
  • ANPR/ALPR Cameras:

    Specialized cameras that read license plates in all lighting conditions.

  • Parking Guidance Sensors:

    Ultrasonic or camera-based sensors installed above each parking bay.

  • LED Wayfinding Displays:

    Dynamic signs that show the number of free spaces on each level/zone.

  • Automated Barrier Gates:

    Heavy-duty barriers for controlling vehicle flow.

  • Payment Kiosks (POF):

    Self-service machines for cash, card, or mobile wallet payments.

Places of Use

  • Shopping malls and retail parks.

  • Airports and transport hubs.

  • Corporate office towers and business parks.

  • Luxury hotels and residential complexes.

  • Hospitals and healthcare centers.
