Intrusion Alarm Systems

Proactive Detection and Instant Response for Complete Peace of Mind

Security starts before an intruder even enters your building. Our Intrusion Alarm Systems are designed to detect unauthorized movement, glass breakage, or forced entry attempts at the perimeter. By combining high-sensitivity sensors with loud sirens and instant mobile notifications, we ensure that you are alerted the moment a threat is detected, allowing for a rapid response to protect your property and loved ones.

System Benefits
  • Early Intrusion Detection:

    Sensors trigger an alarm as soon as a door or window is tampered with.

  • Visual Deterrence:

    The presence of external sirens and window decals discourages potential burglars.

  • 24/7 Mobile Alerts:

    Receive push notifications on your phone whenever the system is armed, disarmed, or triggered.

  • Pet-Friendly Sensors:

    Advanced PIR technology ignores small pets to prevent false alarms.

  • Insurance Savings:

    Many insurance companies offer lower premiums for buildings protected by certified alarm systems.

  • Integration with CCTV:

    Automatically trigger cameras to record when an intrusion alarm is activated.

System Components
  • Main Control Panel:

    The brain of the system that processes all sensor signals and communicates with the network.

  • Motion Detectors (PIR):

    Sensors that detect infrared heat signatures from moving humans.

  • Magnetic Door/Window Contacts:

    Two-piece sensors that trigger when a door or window is opened.

  • Glass Break Detectors:

    Acoustic sensors tuned to the frequency of breaking glass.

  • Keypads and Key Fobs:

    User interfaces to easily arm or disarm the system with a code or button.

Places of Use

  • Residential villas and townhouses.

  • Retail shops and luxury boutiques.

  • Banking and financial institutions.

  • Warehouses and storage facilities.

  • Pharmacies and medical clinics.
