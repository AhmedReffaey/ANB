Master Clock & Central Clock Systems

Synchronization You Can Count On

Our Master Clock and Central Systems provide precise, synchronized timekeeping across entire facilities. Essential for sectors like healthcare, education, and transportation, these systems ensure that all operations run on the exact same schedule, improving efficiency, punctuality, and coordination.

System Benefits
  • Accurate Timekeeping:

    Ensures every clock in the building displays the exact same time, down to the second.

  • Operational Efficiency:

    Eliminates confusion caused by unsynchronized clocks, streamlining shifts, classes, and appointments.

  • Automation:

    Systems can trigger external devices counting bells, lights, or HVAC systems at scheduled times.

  • Low Maintenance:

    Automatic daylight saving time adjustments and battery backup reduce manual intervention.

  • Scalability:

    Easily expand the system to cover new areas or buildings as needed.

  • Unified Control:

    Manage all timekeeping devices from a central interface.

System Components
  • Master Clock Unit:

    The central brain that receives time signals (via GPS or NTP) and distributes them.

  • Secondary Clocks:

    Analog or digital clocks installed throughout the facility that receive signals from the master unit.

  • Signal Transmitters:

    Wireless or wired devices that broadcast time data to secondary clocks.

  • Software Interface:

    Allows administrators to configure settings, schedules, and alarms.

  • GPS Receiver/NTP Server:

    Ensures the master clock stays perfectly accurate by syncing with global time standards.

Places of Use

  • Schools and universities.

  • Hospitals and healthcare facilities.

  • Airports, train stations, and transport hubs.

  • Corporate offices and factories.

  • Governmental institutions.
